# KLAPKA production standard, v1

Approved by Koks on 2026-11-08 after the Sony Ericsson W995 ("looks exactly as it should").
Every phone goes through these steps. Changing the standard needs Koks's yes; experiments are fine,
but they stay experiments until he approves them.

**Principle:** geometry comes from data, appearance comes from a photo, printed text comes from data.

## Deliverables per phone (`phones/<id>/`)

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `phone.json` | the phone's data ([docs/FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md)) |
| `build.json` | how it was made: template, photo and licence, seed scores, text fixes, acceptance |
| `body.png` | master: RGBA, 12 px/mm, 1 mm transparent padding, screen not cut |
| `skin.png` | the master with the screen hole at the exact native aspect |
| `mi-band-192x490.png` | Mi Band 7/8/9 export, transparent around the phone and in the screen |
| `preview.png`, `thumb.png` | page preview (with a sample clock) and gallery thumbnail |
| `README.md` | the phone's page, generated by `klapka.py pages` |
| `work/` | not committed: template, renders with sidecar JSONs (prompt, seed, size, model), crops, sheets |

## Rules

1. **Sliders and flips are always shown open** (keypad slid out, clamshell unfolded):
   `view: "open"`, `size_mm` is the open size, `layout_mm.seams` marks where the parts meet.
   **Exception (Koks, 2026-11-08): phones whose keypad sits on a flip that closes over the
   touchscreen** (Sony Ericsson P800, P900, P910i, P990i) are shown **closed**, because that is how
   the keypad was seen: `view: "closed"`, `size_mm` is the closed size, the screen is the part of the
   display visible above the closed flip (`display.native_px` = that visible area), and
   `layout_mm.seams` marks the flip's edges.
2. **Reference photos** follow `docs/REFERENCES.md` (factory configuration first). Any reasonable
   angle works; nothing has to be frontal. Photos are input only: never published, source and licence
   recorded in `phone.json`.
3. **The screen is drawn at the exact native aspect**, its size from the diagonal; the final hole has
   the exact native aspect too.
4. **Images come from Qwen-Image 2.1 on a stable-diffusion.cpp server** (the maintainer uses KLIF
   System CGI, preset `qwen-image-21`, Turbo INT8): 8 steps, cfg 1.0, euler, one request at a time.
   `tools/backend.py` finds the server.
5. **Canvas sizes are multiples of 32 and at most 1,048,576 px** (704×1408 for ~0.45 phones,
   640×1632 for long sliders). Larger canvases slow sd.cpp down and did not fix text.
6. **Printed text from pass 1 is never trusted.** Every text and key label is read letter by letter;
   anything wrong goes through the text pass (step 5).
7. **Prompts come only from the standard IDs in `tools/prompts.json`**; experiments live in
   `tools/prompts_archive.json`.
9. **Text fixes are minimal:** redraw only what is wrong. Redraws can drift the small icons next to
   key labels, so a correct row is never redrawn.
8. **Prototype phase:** no test suites. Acceptance = the numbers below + Koks's visual approval.

## Steps

`tools/klapka.py` runs each step; the scripts it calls are listed for reference. The runbook with
what to look at after each step is [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

| # | Command | What happens | Underlying tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `new <id> --brand B --model M`, fill `phone.json`, `measure <id> PHOTO` | data: real size (open size for sliders and flips, measured if no spec gives it), display diagonal and resolution, screen top edge, keypad block, seams, texts, keys, sources | – |
| 1 | `template <id>` | layout drawn to scale on the largest canvas that fits (multiples of 32, ≤ 1 MP, phone ~92% of the height); the screen at the exact native aspect | `make_template.py` |
| 2 | `photo <id> PATH --source URL --license L --author A` | appearance photo, EXIF-corrected, longest side 1024 | – |
| 3 | `pass1 <id>` | seeds 1–4: template first, photo second, prompt `koks_template`; ranked relative to the phone's own box: a seed passes with a white background, body aspect within 3% and the screen centred within 1%; among passing seeds the best screen match and left/right symmetry win; overlay sheet | `cgi_edit.py`, `score_render.py` |
| 4 | `pass2 <id>` | background removal on the best seed (prompt `cutout`); moves nothing | `cgi_edit.py` |
| 5 | `grid <id>`, `rows <id>`, `textfix <id> --row N` / `--keypad` / `--text T --box …` | every printed text read; only the wrong areas (a row of keys, preferred; the whole keypad only when most rows are wrong; a word) cut out, enlarged to an exact multiple of 32, redrawn with the exact wording (`fix_key_row` / `fix_keypad_tpl` / `fix_text`), pasted back colour-matched, alpha untouched | `fix_text.py`, `cgi_edit.py` |
| 6 | `finish <id>` | master `body.png` (12 px/mm), screen hole at the exact native aspect (`skin.png`), Mi Band export, preview, thumbnail, measured acceptance | `clean_alpha.py`, `mark_screen.py` |
| 7 | `mark <id> --text-ok` (and `--approved` by the maintainer) | the visual checks recorded in `build.json` | – |
| 8 | `pages` | phone pages; the catalog (`catalog/`: by year, by brand, by type, all phones); the front page (newest additions, browse links); `catalog.json` | – |
| – | `header` (maintainer) | the README header animation from the hand-picked line-up in `assets/header.json` | `make_header.py` |

If no seed passes (white background, aspect within 3%), run `pass1 <id> --seeds 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8`.
To restart the text fixes from the clean cutout: `pass2 <id> --reuse`.

## Acceptance

Pass/fail (`finish` prints `PASS` only when all of these hold):

| Check | Threshold |
|---|---|
| body aspect vs spec | within ±3% (Koks, 2026-11-08: renders land 2–4% off; ≤3% is acceptable) |
| lean (mean angle of the two sides, after the automatic straightening) | ≤ 0.3° |
| screen hole aspect vs native | error ≤ 0.2% |
| printed text | every letter right, icons plausible (`mark --text-ok`) |
| text-pass seams | none visible at 2× |
| maintainer | visual approval (`mark --approved`) |

Measured and recorded in `build.json`, for information only (Koks, 2026-11-08): taper (sides
converging), side tilt, edge jitter and left/right symmetry. Rounded or tapering sides and buttons on
one side are part of the design, so these are not pass/fail.

## Tried and rejected (do not retry without a new idea)

| Approach | Why it lost |
|---|---|
| Cut the background out of the photo | keeps perspective, noise, wear and soft labels; 1 px brown rim from the old background |
| Qwen "re-render" / "front view" prompts on the photo alone | the edit keeps the photo's layout: perspective and visible sides stay |
| Perspective rectification (4 corners + homography) | fixes tilt and keystone, not a visible side face |
| Second reference image with a sharp keypad | helps only when such a photo exists; not general |
| 2 MP canvases (1024×2048) | sharper letters, digits fixed, letters still wrong; ~3 min per image |
| Labels drawn into the template | partly right, and one render copied the flat template, ignoring the photo |

## What the first batch taught (2026-11-08, seven Sony Ericssons)

- The render keeps a little of the photo's viewing angle: an angled photo gave a K700i with one side
  panel showing and the screen off-centre. Seeds are now also ranked by screen centring and mirror
  symmetry, and frontal photos are preferred.
- Lean is fixed automatically in `finish` (T610 0.35°, K800i 0.39° before; ~0° after).
- Key rows must be found on the render, not taken from the template: template rows landed on the
  wrong keys and duplicated rows.
- Redrawing a whole keypad fixes letters but drifts icons; fix rows, `restore` the right ones.
- A wrong glyph tends to survive repeated text fixes; another passing seed is the fix.
- Sister models share a chassis, not necessarily a keypad position (K750i vs W800i).
- Spec sheets can be wrong (T610 diagonal 1.9″ does not fit its glass; 1.8″ does).
- The automatic screen hole can miss a display that shows content; the overlay must be checked, and
  `--screen` gives it by hand.
- Rounded or tapering sides and one-sided buttons are design, so taper, edge jitter and symmetry are
  recorded but not pass/fail (Koks).

## Open items

- Export to real watch face formats (per band generation) is not started.
